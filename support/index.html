Support

We’re here to help with OpenBook.

For account help, bank connections, bugs, or privacy requests, email jaykpatel.dev@gmail.com.

Please include your OpenBook account email, the business name if relevant, and a short description of what you’re seeing. Screenshots help for bank-connect or sync issues.

Common topics

  • Signing in or resetting your password
  • Inviting a co-owner or ownership changes
  • Connecting or disconnecting a bank account via Plaid
  • Missing or delayed transactions after sync
  • Deleting a business or requesting account deletion

Bank connections

OpenBook uses Plaid to connect accounts. We never see your bank password. If a connection fails at the bank login screen, try again later or contact your bank; if it fails after linking, email us with the institution name and approximate time.
